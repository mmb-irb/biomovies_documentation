# Tips and shortcuts

## Making good movies

* **From the whole to the detail**: start with the whole structure, then fly to what the movie is about (a pocket, a
  ligand, a mutation, an interface) with a transition.
* **One idea per clip**, 3 to 6 seconds each; transitions of 1 to 2 seconds. **Cloned Clip After** is the usual way to
  go on: change only what differs, and a transition animates the change.
* **Keep the context, faded**: the rest of the protein at an opacity of 20 to 40 while the subject is solid, in a
  colour of its own (see [Representations](representations.html)).
* **Gentle cameras**: a turn of 30 to 90 degrees during a clip gives the sense of 3D; more than about 120 is too fast.
* **Short texts**: a title under 8 words, a description under 12, one at a time.
* **A dark background** makes the colours stand out; a light one suits print.
* **End with a fade out** of about a second.
* Turn on the **Video frame** tool before setting the cameras, so that nothing important falls outside the video.

## Working with biomovies

* **Save often**: biomovies is in its alpha version. The status bar says when there are unsaved changes, and closing a
  project with changes asks first.
* **Undo** (⌘Z, Ctrl+Z) undoes any change, a whole assistant's request included.
* **Look around** without changing any keyframe with **Explore** (see [The stage](editor-stage.html#exploring-the-stage)).
* **Open projects** from the start screen's **Recent projects**, or double-click a **.biomovies** or **.tbiomovies**
  file.
* **Share** a movie as a **.biomovies** file (with the structure and the trajectory inside), or as a template
  (**.tbiomovies**: small, without them).

## Big projects

A project with hundreds of representations, or a structure of millions of atoms in many clips, would not fit in the
computer's memory if every clip were kept on the stage. biomovies then switches to the **memory saving mode** by
itself, and a message says so: only the clips, transitions and fades near the cursor are kept on the stage, and the
others are prepared as the cursor gets near them. Playing waits for them (*Preparing…* on the stage); the exported
videos are the same.

## Frequent questions

**I drag on the stage and the camera does not change.** The mouse moves a clip's camera only with **one clip keyframe
selected** in the timeline (click it). With the cursor between keyframes, the stage shows the movie but keeps its
camera; to look around anyway, use **Explore**.

**+ New > Transition says something is missing.** Select the last keyframe of a clip and Shift+click the first
keyframe of the next one, with at least 0.4 s between them (drag the second clip to the right to make room).

**A selection matches no atoms.** Residue numbers and chain names are the file's own, which may differ from a
paper's. The **Sequences** tool shows the chains and their numbering.

**pLDDT (confidence) is greyed out.** The structure is experimental, or its file has no confidence scores: the reason
is under the scheme's name in the list.

**The video shows less than the stage at the sides.** Videos are 16:9: turn on the **Video frame** tool to see what
they leave out.

**My computer warns about biomovies when installing it.** See [Installation](installation.html): biomovies is not
signed with a paid certificate, so the system asks you to confirm the first time.

**Something went wrong.** Undo, save the project under a new name, and tell us with **Help > Report Issue**.

### Other keys and gestures

| Where | Keys | What they do |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Stage | Drag, right-drag, scroll | Turn, move, zoom (the camera of the selected clip keyframe). |
| Stage | Alt+click two atoms | A distance. |
| Stage | Alt+right-click three atoms | An angle. |
| Stage | Alt+Shift+click an atom | A label. |
| Stage | Esc | Leaves **Explore**. |
| Timeline | Shift+click | Adds a keyframe or an item to the selection. |
| Timeline | Double-click a bar, or ⌘+click / Ctrl+click | Selects the whole item. |
| Timeline | Ctrl+scroll, or a trackpad pinch | Zooms the timeline. |
| Trajectory window | ⌘⌥1 / ⌘⌥2 (Ctrl+Alt+1 / 2) | The range's first frame down / up. |
| Trajectory window | ⌘⌥3 / ⌘⌥4 (Ctrl+Alt+3 / 4) | The range's last frame down / up. |
| Sequences | Click, drag, Shift+click | Adds or removes residues of the selected representation. |
| Export window | Alt while dragging a handle | Puts it anywhere, not only at an item's edge. |
| An assistant working | Esc | Unlocks the editor once you have stopped the assistant. |

On macOS, Alt is the **⌥ Option** key.
